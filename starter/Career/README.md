# Your Career folder

Keep this folder on your computer or in Google Drive. The numbers keep things in order.

| Folder | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| 01_profile | `skills.md`, your master profile. The source of truth. |
| 02_resumes | One base resume per kind of role, your safe copy for AI, and a corrections log |
| 03_linkedin | Your headline, About section and experience text |
| 04_stories | Your interview stories |
| 05_rules | Your writing likes and names you can't use publicly |
| jobs | One folder per job you apply to, plus the tracker |

**For each new job:** copy `jobs/_COPY-ME_Company_Role`, rename it (for example `Acme_Support-Lead`), and start a new chat with the same name inside your Career Kit Project.

**Upload to your AI Project:** `01_profile/skills.md`, your base resume, your LinkedIn text, `04_stories/story-bank.md` and `05_rules/rules.md`. That's 5 files, the free ChatGPT limit.
